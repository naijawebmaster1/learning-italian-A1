Italian Indefinite Articles · A1 Grammar

What is the Indefinite Article?

Like "a" or "an" in English — used for a non-specific person or thing.

In Italian, indefinite articles are used in the singular only. They agree with the gender and the first letter of the noun. There is no plural form (instead, we use partitive articles like dei, delle, which you'll learn later!).

♂ Maschile (Masculine)

ArticleRuleExample
unconsonants & vowelsun libro, un amico

♀ Femminile (Feminine)

ArticleRuleExample
unaall consonantsuna casa, una ragazza
un'vowels onlyun'idea, un'amica

Key Insights

⚠️ No Apostrophe for Masc

Masc. un never takes an apostrophe, even before a vowel. un amico (Correct) vs un'amico (Wrong!).

✨ The Apostrophe for Fem

Fem. una becomes un' only before a vowel. un'isola, un'arancia.
